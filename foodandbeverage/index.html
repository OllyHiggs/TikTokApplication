01 — Tesco Media and Insight Platform

The UK's largest grocer, at scale.

Tesco Media and Insight Platform is the retail media arm of Tesco, owned and operated by dunnhumby — Tesco's data science subsidiary. With 24 million Clubcard households and around 27% of UK grocery market share, it is the most scaled closed-loop retail media network in Britain. The dunnhumby Sphere platform gives brands self-serve access to campaign planning, activation and closed-loop measurement across both online and in-store.

Tesco · Media and Insight Platform

Powered by dunnhumby

The Tesco Media and Insight Platform connects brands to 24 million UK Clubcard households — the UK's largest loyalty dataset — giving closed-loop visibility across every Clubcard transaction online and in-store. dunnhumby Sphere, the self-serve activation layer, allows brands to plan, buy and measure retail media campaigns without managed-service overhead.

With around 27% of UK grocery market share, Tesco's first-party data audience is structurally larger than any other UK grocer. For brands selling alcohol through Tesco, including Diageo's portfolio, this is the only UK retail media platform where Clubcard data enables attribution from a TikTok creator impression through to a verifiable Tesco purchase — both online and at the till.

Approval and alcohol compliance

Two approval layers. Alcohol rules met at delivery.

Every piece of creator content placed on either surface has been pre-cleared through the existing Spark Ads authorisation flow, restricted to creators authorised for 18+ audiences. On top of that base, surface-specific approval routes apply, and TikTok's own UK alcohol advertising conditions govern what can run and how.

Two-layer approval

Spark Ads authorisation. Then surface-specific sign-off.

The content pool for both surfaces is gated to creators who have been authorised by Diageo through the Spark Ads flow — restricted to the subset of those creators explicitly approved for 18+ audiences. That gate is the floor. Everything else sits on top of it.

On the Spirits category and search pages, the unit is a brand-funded ad placement. Diageo approves which creator content runs, because Diageo is the advertiser funding the spend. Approval can be asset-by-asset or via pre-authorised creator lists with automated content-quality rules where Diageo has configured those.

On the Pimm's can PDP, the unit is real estate Tesco owns. Tesco approves which Spark-authorised content appears in the product gallery for that SKU — the same approval mechanic, but a different approver, because a different party owns the surface. Either party can revoke at any time. Creators retain takedown rights over their own content.

TikTok UK alcohol advertising conditions

The live requirements, as applied to both surfaces.

TikTok's own UK alcohol policy¹ sets the conditions any alcohol advertiser must meet to run on the platform. Those conditions apply here in full. Each is met by the design of the two surfaces:

  • Restricted to users 18+. The sponsored category unit targets only users TikTok has verified or inferred as 18+ in the UK. The PDP gallery does not target users — it is a Tesco-owned surface where Tesco's own age-verification at checkout already applies.
  • Landing page age-gating. Tesco age-verifies all alcohol purchases at checkout — customers must confirm they are 18+. That mechanism is already in place and is used as the age-gating mechanism for both surfaces on this plan.
  • Compliance with applicable laws, regulations and codes. Both surfaces comply with applicable UK law. Under TikTok's requirement to comply with applicable codes sit the UK ASA and CAP rules and the Portman Group Code of Practice. Those codes set the standards the creative must meet; TikTok's policy is the delivery gate through which compliant creative must pass.
  • No offer, prize or discount on the creative that incentivises consumption. The sponsored category unit carries no price, no promotional offer and no discount. The PDP gallery video is creator content only — product information and pricing are shown separately by Tesco in the standard info panel.
  • No depiction of irresponsible drinking. Creator content is reviewed against this condition during the Spark Ads authorisation process. Content that depicts irresponsible drinking cannot be authorised.
  • No implication that alcohol improves health, attractiveness or social success. Applied as a content moderation rule during Spark authorisation. Rejected at the pool level before it reaches either surface.
  • Human eligibility gate via TikTok Sales Representative. TikTok requires alcohol advertisers to work with a TikTok Sales Representative to determine eligibility before running alcohol advertising. That representative review is a real human gate that precedes any live campaign — it is part of the approval story, not a formality.
